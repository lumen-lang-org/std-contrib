Kaffa: The Birthplace of Coffee

The Legend of Kaldi and the Dancing Goats

The story of coffee begins in the ancient forests of the Kaffa region in Ethiopia. According to legend, a goat herder named Kaldi discovered the coffee plant after noticing that his goats became so energetic they "danced" after eating the bright red berries from a particular tree. Kaldi reported his findings to the abbot of the local monastery, who made a drink with the berries and found that it kept him alert through the long hours of evening prayer. The abbot shared his discovery with the other monks, and the knowledge of the energizing berries began to spread.

While the story of Kaldi may be folklore, it is believed that the forests of Kaffa are the original home of the Arabica coffee plant. The region remains a vital center for coffee production and a place of pilgrimage for coffee lovers from around the world.
